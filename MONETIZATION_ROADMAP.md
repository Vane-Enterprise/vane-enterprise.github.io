# Monetization Roadmap for Vane Enterprise

## Objective
Turn the public repository and landing site into a credible revenue-generating digital asset without overstating legal or sponsorship claims. The project should combine public trust, technical credibility, and a structured offering model.

## Revenue Model Overview

### 1. Community Funding via Open Collective
Use the Open Collective arrangement as the main recurring support layer.

Priority actions:
- Add a clear "Support This Project" page
- Publish donation tiers and funding breakdown
- Add a basic explanation of how funds are used
- Keep funding language limited to "fiscal host" and "community support"

### 2. Professional Services Around the Product
The strongest monetization path is not just donations. It is service and advisory work built around the project mission.

Offer services such as:
- AI governance and architecture review
- deterministic AI workflow planning
- secure RAG pipeline design review
- zero-trust architecture consulting
- enterprise AI governance workshops
- technical trust and compliance documentation support

### 3. Corporate Sponsorship / Strategic Partnership Layer
Once credibility grows, establish a formal strategic partnership path.

Examples:
- Corporate Patron level
- Strategic Partnership support
- Advisory board and technical review engagement
- Co-branded educational or research initiatives

### 4. Education and Training
Package technical education and applied training around the project theme.

Examples:
- AI governance bootcamps
- secure architecture workshops
- trust-layer implementation sessions
- enterprise AI risk and reliability briefings

## Recommended 90-Day Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1–30)
- Finalize public trust messaging
- Add support page and funding tiers
- Confirm Open Collective profile and funding copy
- Publish a clear mission and governance statement
- Create visible support and donation call-to-action on homepage

### Phase 2: Revenue Activation (Days 31–60)
- Start public support campaign
- Launch a minimal sponsor package
- Publish a project roadmap and use-of-funds summary
- Add service offerings for consulting and advisory work
- Create a simple contact flow for interested organizations

### Phase 3: Conversion and Partnerships (Days 61–90)
- Reach out to aligned organizations and institutions
- Offer paid advisory sessions and workshop packages
- Add recognition and supporter notices on the website
- Build a recurring monthly support campaign via Open Collective
- Publish quarterly trust and project progress notes

## Suggested Offer Structure

### Public Support
- Community Supporter: $10/month
- Project Backer: $25/month
- Trusted Builder: $99/month

### Corporate Offerings
- Corporate Patron: $250/month
- Strategic Partner: $1,000+/month
- Consulting engagement packages: $500–$5,000+

## Best Practices
- Keep the project positioning credible and professional
- Separate donations from legal ownership claims
- Be explicit that the project is supported by a fiscal host arrangement
- Maintain a public roadmap and regular updates
- Turn site traffic into consulting leads with a strong service page

## Expected Outcome
Within a 3–6 month period, the project should become a credible combination of:
- public trust asset
- funding vehicle via Open Collective
- service-led revenue stream
- strategic partnership opportunity

This is the best path to turning the repo from a static landing page into a sustainable public-interest and commercial ecosystem.
